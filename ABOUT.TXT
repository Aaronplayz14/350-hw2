Implementation notes
--------------------
- Build and launch instructions and controls are in README.md.
- docs/RESOURCE_USAGE.md explains ownership, resource access, and lifetimes.
- Systems borrow managers through EngineView. ECSEngine owns the managers;
  EntityManager owns components; SoundManager/SpriteManager own loaded media.
- The scene loads sky.map before world.map. Type-1 dictionaries may omit
  collision rectangles; type-2 entries supply local collision bounds.
- Spike respawning is enabled: lowercase 'x' spike/saw tiles and uppercase
  'X' tiles carry damage flags.
  Spike bounds are clipped to the drawn tile, removing the dictionary's
  one-pixel overhang that otherwise creates invisible wall-side ledges.
  Contact returns the player to its configured starting spawn with zero velocity.
- Spawner tiles are solid to the player and ignored by stars. The current
  spawn budget is finite (five events per spawner); timing is frame-based.
- Stars launch at 3-5 world units/frame, accelerate downward by 0.6, and have
  a downward speed cap of 12. Floor rebound retains 28% of normal speed and
  ground friction retains 78% of horizontal speed per floor resolution.
  Small velocities become zero, while gravity keeps checking floor support.
- Camera follow retains Travis's hard/soft bands. Shake uses an independent
  display offset, a four-world-unit amplitude cap, and a 14-frame fade.
- Contact snapshots run before input/movement; collision resolution follows
  movement. Spawning currently runs before score/camera/render, so a new star
  can be drawn immediately and starts physics on the next simulation frame.
- Diagonal collisions use the later axis-entry time. Existing floor/ceiling
  support is preserved across coplanar tile seams, and resolved positions snap
  to the contacted surface so landing does not create false wall contacts.
- Component structs are grouped in a few headers (for example, movement,
  gravity, and respawn in LocationComponent.h); they are not all separate files.

Verification and remaining manual checks
---------------------------------------
Build with the warning flags configured by CMake and run build/engine/ECSTest
directly. The test program includes resource-failure cases, which intentionally
print missing-file diagnostics. CTest has no registered tests in this project.
Local checks do not replace building/running on the lab machines, playing the
provided traversal sections, listening to event sounds, or judging camera feel.
The collision checks do not establish general continuous collision detection
for arbitrary speeds, all corner configurations, or every possible map.
