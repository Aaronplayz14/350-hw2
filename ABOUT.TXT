Implementation notes
--------------------
- README.md gives build/launch instructions, controls, and the test command.
  docs/RESOURCE_USAGE.md documents resource ownership, access, and lifetimes.
- ECSEngine owns the managers. Systems borrow them through EngineView;
  EntityManager owns components and the media managers own loaded resources.
- The scene loads sky.map for the background and world.map for objects. The
  supplied map layouts are unchanged. The extra star starts at (576, 192),
  outside the left wall, and uses ordinary star physics.
- All component definitions have dedicated headers. CameraFollower and
  CameraShake are compatibility aliases for CameraFollowerComponent and
  CameraShakeComponent.
- The frame cap is 30 FPS. Movement/timers are frame-based. The dash cooldown
  is 30 frames, dash duration 15 frames, and wall-slide cap 3 before gravity.
- Spawners are solid to the player but ignored by stars. Each spawner has a
  five-event budget and emits stars in random directions. Stars lose energy
  on collisions and settle on floors; collection adds 10 points.
- Spike/saw x and uppercase X tiles respawn the player at its starting
  location. Spike hitboxes are clipped to the drawn tile to avoid overhangs.
- Collision snapshots precede movement; resolution follows it. Ground/wall
  contacts are maintained across tile seams. Spawning runs after sprite drawing.
- The camera keeps a fixed base y, follows horizontally with hard/soft bands,
  and applies a fading directional shake on player impacts.
- SpriteSystem draws into the back buffer. WindowManager converts debug boxes
  from world coordinates, and the engine presents once after drawing overlays.
